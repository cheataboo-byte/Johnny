Privacy notice

Plain-language notice for this project. This is a product disclosure, not legal advice. The site owner must review it for their organization, Firebase region, applicable laws, and contact details before public launch.

Information this app can store

  • Firebase Authentication account email, display name, and authentication metadata for real accounts.
  • Optional profile preferences: broad age range, optional gender, skin type, selected learning concerns, and ingredient allergy/sensitivity notes. The app asks for separate consent before saving concerns or allergy notes.
  • Product names, parsed ingredient lists, saved ingredients, bot settings, language, and notification preferences/history.
  • If you enable browser push, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a device token and this app stores it under your account so the server can deliver updates to that device.
  • Demo mode stores its profile only in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes that local demo data.

Why and where information is used

Profile data is used for the requested account sync and transparent, rule-based educational suggestions. Gender is not used to rank suggestions. The Firebase project owner chooses the Firebase project and its data region; Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions, and their service providers process data under their own terms.

If email notifications are enabled, only verified account addresses are eligible. The deployed server function uses Resend to deliver owner-published announcements. Resend will process the recipient address and message. The app does not send profile concerns or allergy notes in notifications.

Chat messages and recent-message context are kept in browser memory and are not sent to an AI service or saved as chat transcripts. For an unmatched chemistry or product question, the app may send an extracted compound name to NIH PubChem or a short topic (not the full question or chat history) to Wikipedia's public API. These lookups require internet access; avoid entering personal or sensitive information.

The website loads DM Sans and Noto Sans Khmer from Google Fonts when online; the browser may disclose standard connection metadata to Google Fonts. Local/system fonts are fallbacks.

Choices, export, and deletion

Profile details are optional. You can turn notifications off, remove saved health-related notes, export account data, or request account deletion in Settings. Firebase account deletion requires a recent sign-in and deletes the account's Firestore data and Authentication user. Provider or operational logs may have their own retention periods.

Important limits

Do not enter medication, emergency, or identifying medical-record information. This app is not a medical record, allergy checker, diagnostic system, or health-care provider. An exact label-name match cannot confirm that a product contains or is free of an allergen.
