An open notebook

How the algorithms work.

No mystery scores: here's what the local analyzer and recommender look at, and where their limits are.

01 · Name normalization

Common names and spelling variants map to a canonical ingredient name before lookup (for example, Vitamin B3 → Niacinamide). Ingredient labels may use different INCI conventions; unknown names are kept visible rather than discarded.

input → trim / lowercase → alias map → knowledge lookup

02 · Biochemistry knowledge base

Each built-in entry describes a broad chemical class, a simplified mechanism, formulation context, cautions, and practical label-reading notes. It is educational, not a measurement of product efficacy or safety.

03 · Interaction rules

Explicit, readable if/else rules look for potentially complementary groups and commonly irritating combinations. These flags are context prompts, not proof of incompatibility: concentration, product pH, formulation, schedule, and individual tolerance matter.

  • Humectants + barrier lipids → complementary hydration/barrier roles
  • Strong exfoliating acids + retinoids → irritation caution

04 · Recommendation scoring

The recommender counts ingredient classes in saved scans, compares them with complementary classes, and adds a small focus-match bonus. Repeated high-caution surfactant flags trigger a formula alert. No profile data is sent anywhere.

score = missing complementary class + focus relevance − already-used class

05 · Storage & privacy

Demo accounts, scans, preferences, and bot settings are stored in this browser's localStorage. Data can be cleared by removing this site's local storage. This demo does not provide real authentication or encrypted cloud sync.

Publishing: Replace the marked AuthManager storage adapter with Firebase Authentication and Firestore rules; never treat client-only localStorage as secure account storage.

06 · Limits & professional care

The knowledge base is intentionally compact and cannot verify an actual product's complete formula, concentration, purity, claims, or regulatory status. Do not use this app to diagnose or to start, stop, or replace treatment. Ask a qualified professional for personalized guidance.
