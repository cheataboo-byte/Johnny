Terms of use

Draft for an educational project; not legal advice. The operator must review and adapt these terms before public launch.

Educational use only

molecule lab provides simplified ingredient and formulation information for learning. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, product certification, or an allergy-safety service. Ingredient data may be incomplete or out of date. Do not rely on this app to make treatment decisions or delay professional care.

Ask a qualified professional

Consult a dermatologist, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, allergies, pregnancy, prescribed treatment, or personal product use. Seek appropriate urgent care for an emergency. Stop using a product and seek professional advice if you are concerned about a reaction.

Accounts and notifications

Real accounts require the operator's configured Firebase project. Push and email delivery depend on browser permission and properly configured third-party services. Demo mode is local-only and is not secure cloud authentication. Do not share passwords or submit sensitive details you do not want stored.

Use responsibly

Do not use the service to submit unlawful content, probe another person's account, or send unsolicited messages. Suggestions are exploratory examples, not endorsements or prescriptions.
